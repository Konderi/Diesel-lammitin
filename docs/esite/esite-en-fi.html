HeatCall

Control your diesel heater by text message
Android app compatible with Eberspächer EasyStart Call

The manufacturer's own EasyStart Call app was discontinued and no longer works on new Android phones. HeatCall brings back remote control of your heater with a familiar look and new features.

Start with one tap
Run time and mode on familiar wheels

Replies in plain language
The heater's message opens in its own window

Home screen widget
Status and check without opening the app

Features

1
Start and stopRun time 10–120 min or continuous for air heaters, heating or ventilation.
2
Always know the statusCheck the status from the card or the widget. Error codes are explained.
3
TimersThree weekly timers with departure time, e.g. Mon–Fri at 07:30.
4
Device clockSet it from the phone time with one tap, with a reminder when summer or winter time begins.
5
No clutterReplies are automatically marked as read in your messaging app.
6
PrivateNo internet connection, ads or tracking. Your data stays on your phone.

How it works
1
Enter the heater detailsThe phone number of the EasyStart Call SIM card and the four-digit PIN. You can add several cars.
2
Choose and pressChoose the run time and mode and press START. The command is sent by text message in the background.
3
Get the replyThe heater confirms, and HeatCall shows the status in plain language and updates the widget.

Timers

Device clock

What you need

  • An Eberspächer heater (Hydronic or Airtronic) with an EasyStart Call unit
  • An active SIM card in the unit
  • An Android phone, Android 10 or newer
  • Every command is a text message that your operator may charge for
  • Start confirmations require the unit's confirmation messages to be on. Status queries are always answered.

Tested

  • Google Pixel and OPPO (ColorOS), with instructions for Samsung and Xiaomi too
  • Brand-specific guidance for background activity and permission settings
